# The frozen bet — scoring rules, pre-registered

*Frozen with this repository's `freeze-2026-27` tag, September
2026, before NHL opening night. These rules do not change after
the freeze; in April the only new input is the season itself.*

## What is frozen

`data/projections_2026_27.csv` — our projected 2026-27 value
(per-82) for the 661 skaters with 30+ GP in 2025-26. It is
generated by `harness/freeze_2026_27.py` from data shipped in
this repository, deterministically: rerun the script and diff
the file to verify the freeze is exactly the stated recipe
(the tournament's own Marcel, launched from 2025-26) and
nothing else.

## When

Grading runs after the 2026-27 NHL regular season ends, in
April 2027. Results publish at pcoastlabs.com whatever they say.

## How the table is graded

1. **Judges**: each entrant's ACTUAL 2026-27 season values, from
   the same sources and converters as this repository (skaters,
   30+ GP in 2026-27, per-82). Our own actual 2026-27 values are
   computed by the same pipeline that produced
   `data/ours_values.csv` and are published with the grading.
2. **Predictors**:
   - our frozen projection (the file above, unchanged);
   - each entrant's raw 2025-26 value;
   - each entrant's Marcel built from data through 2025-26 by
     this repository's recipe — every metric faces its own
     monkey, prospectively this time;
   - any projection an entrant themselves published before the
     2026-27 season started, where a pre-season timestamp is
     verifiable.
3. **Protocol**: identical to the retrospective tournament —
   per-82, 30-game minimums in both seasons, strict common
   sample across every predictor and judge column, Spearman
   rank correlation per cell.
4. **The whole grid publishes.** No cell is dropped.

## Named in advance

The comparisons we commit to reporting, chosen now so nobody
selects them after seeing the season:

- our frozen projection against our own 2026-27 values, and the
  margin over the best rival on that column;
- our frozen projection's row mean against every entrant's
  Marcel row mean;
- the prospective monkey law, for every entrant including us:
  raw 2025-26 against its own Marcel, on its own scoreboard.
  If our raw value out-predicts our frozen projection, the
  monkey beat us, and that gets published in those words.

## Joining

Any public metric can join before the 2026-27 season starts:
normalize to the entrant CSV spec, or publish your own
projection with a verifiable pre-season timestamp and it enters
as a predictor in April. After opening night, the field is
closed for this cycle.

## Format drift

If a source changes its export format between now and April,
the April writeup documents the change and the conversion used.
Drift affects ingestion only; frozen files, the protocol, and
these rules do not change.
